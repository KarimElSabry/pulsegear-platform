# What to do with these metrics to grow the business

Grounded in the numbers found in the audit (49 products available, 82 sold, 23 ledger sales, 23 product requests, 8 accounts).

1. **Close the ledger gap first.** 82 products are marked sold but only 23 sales are recorded, so earnings, margin and every decision below rest on roughly a quarter of the data. Spend one session entering the missing sales (price, cost, commission). Everything else is guesswork until that is done.
2. **Make ordering frictionless.** 33 of 49 available products had a dead order button. With the WhatsApp/Instagram buttons live, track clicks (Phase 3) and compare clicks to sales per product. If clicks are high and sales are low, the problem is follow-up speed or price, not traffic.
3. **Respond to leads within an hour.** Telegram alerts for requests and reservations now make this possible. Add a rule: every new request is marked Contacted the same day. The Requests dashboard filters make unanswered ones visible.
4. **Know your real margin per category.** Once cost snapshots exist, rank products by gross profit, not revenue, and decide what to source more of and which deals to stop.
5. **Price from cost plus a target margin, not from the market only.** Store cost on each product and flag any listing below your target margin (for example 20% of revenue) before it goes live.
6. **Use requests as a demand signal.** The 23 requests show what people want and cannot find. Source the most-requested models in advance and message the people who asked.
7. **Run ads only when you can measure them.** Do not spend on ads until spend is recorded and UTM links are in place. Start small, one campaign per product group, and judge by cost per completed order and contribution after ads, not ROAS alone.
8. **Fix funnel leaks in order:** visits, product views, WhatsApp/reservation, completed order. Act where the biggest drop is: photos and price if views are high but clicks low; response time if clicks are high but orders low.
9. **Grow an owned audience.** Check Admin → Audience weekly. Push the newsletter and account sign-up on product pages; wishlist alerts already exist and are a strong re-engagement tool.
10. **Reduce failed deliveries and returns** (Phase 2 tracks them). Confirm address and phone on WhatsApp before dispatch and take a deposit on high-value orders.
11. **Keep cash and profit separate.** Review collected cash weekly and operating profit monthly. Deposits on sourcing deals are not profit until delivery.
