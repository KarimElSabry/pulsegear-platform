# How to use the Pulse Gear reports (plain-language guide)

Written for the shop owner. No accounting knowledge needed. Keep it open next to the admin pages.

---

## 1. The one rule to remember

**Revenue is what customers paid. Profit is what is left after your costs. Ad spend comes out of profit, not out of revenue.**

Only **completed** sales count. Reservations, cancelled orders and failed deliveries never count. Cash-on-delivery counts only once it is **delivered**.

---

## 2. Where everything is (Admin menu)

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Analytics > Sales Performance** | The dashboard. Key figures, charts, campaign table. This is where you look. |
| **Analytics > Reports** | The same numbers as downloadable reports (CSV, Excel, printable PDF). |
| **Analytics > Finance** | Where you ENTER costs, refunds, expenses and sale sources (7 small forms). |
| **Analytics > Ad Spend** | Meta spend (automatic) or manual entry. |
| **Audience** | Accounts, newsletter, searchable customer list. |
| **Reservations / Requests / Deals** | Day-to-day work with customers. |

## 3. How to read the Key Figures (Sales Performance)

Read the cards from top to bottom like a story:

1. **Net Sales Revenue**: money that came in (after discounts and refunds).
2. **Gross Profit**: revenue minus what the products cost you (includes Germany shipping and Egypt shipping if you entered them on the deal).
3. **Shipping & Fulfillment Costs**: packaging, delivery, payment fees you entered per order. **If it shows 0.00, you have not entered any. It does not mean you paid nothing.**
4. **Contribution Before Ads**: gross profit minus those order costs.
5. **Advertising Spend**: what ads cost in the period.
6. **Contribution After Ads**: **the headline number.** What is left after products, order costs and ads.
7. **Operating Profit**: contribution after ads minus your monthly expenses (rent, tools...). Equals the line above when no expenses are entered.
8. **Net Profit**: after tax and interest. It stays blank (—) until expenses are entered. That is intentional: the page will not guess.
9. **Average Order Value**: revenue divided by completed orders.
10. **Cost per Completed Order**: ad spend divided by completed orders.
11. **ROAS**: revenue divided by ad spend (2.0x means 2 EGP of revenue per 1 EGP of ads). It is a revenue ratio, not profit.
12. **Refund Rate**: share of revenue refunded.

The arrows compare with the previous period of the same length. Green is good, red is bad (for costs, down is green).

### Is it a loss?
Look at **Contribution After Ads** (and **Net Profit** once expenses are entered). Positive means you earned money. Negative means you lost money in that period.

### The date range
At the top, choose *this month, last 7, last 30, last 90, this year* or *custom* (From and To dates). For everything since you started, use **custom** with your first sale date and today.

## 3b. Net result so far, euros and milestones (step 6)

- **Net sales revenue** is always shown at the top, in EGP and euros, whatever costs you entered.
- **Net result so far** always shows a number too: revenue minus the costs you have entered. A yellow line tells you what is still missing, so you know whether it is final. When nothing is missing it equals Net Profit.
- **Admin > Milestones**: type a target in euros (for example 5,000) and watch the bar fill. At 100% you get the celebration. Archive it and set the next one.
- Euros use the live rate (refreshed hourly) unless you type your own rate on the milestone.

## 4. What you must enter, and when

You do **not** fill everything. Only this:

| When | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Once, 5 minutes | Finance, form 7 | Default cost of the products you sell often |
| On every deal | Deals form | Item price, **Shipping from Germany (EUR)**, **Shipping in Egypt (EGP)**, exchange rate, selling price. The form shows total cost and expected profit live. |
| When it happens | Finance, form 1 | Sale cancelled, cash-on-delivery not delivered yet, or delivery failed |
| When it happens | Finance, form 3 | Extra cost of one order (packaging, failed delivery) |
| When it happens | Finance, form 4 | Customer refund or return |
| When you know | Finance, form 5 | Where a sale came from, with a written note (example: "customer said he saw the boosted post") |
| Once a month | Finance, form 6 | Rent, tools, salaries, tax... (needed only for Net Profit) |
| Automatic | Ad Spend | Meta spend syncs every day. If not connected, type it in. |

## 5. Weekly routine (10-15 minutes, same day each week)

1. Open **Sales Performance**, range = *last 7* (and compare with the arrows).
2. Read: **Net Sales Revenue**, **Contribution After Ads**, **Cost per Completed Order**.
3. Look at the campaign table: any campaign with spend and **0 orders**?
4. Open **Finance** and enter any costs, refunds or cancellations from the week.
5. Check the yellow **data quality** warnings below the cards. Fix what they list first.
6. Decide (section 6). Write the decision and the number behind it in a notebook.

## 6. Simple decision rules

- Campaign spent more than twice your usual cost per order and brought **no** reservation: **pause it.**
- Campaign with the lowest cost per order: **raise its budget by 20%** (not more), check again next week.
- Gross margin falling for 2 weeks: check supplier prices, shipping costs, discounts.
- Refund rate above 5%: look at which products and why.
- Cash-on-delivery failures rising: ask for a deposit.
- Do not judge a campaign with fewer than about 30 clicks or 3 leads. The numbers are too noisy.

## 7. The Reports page (downloads)

Choose the date range, then download as **CSV** (for any tool), **Excel**, or **Printable** (open it, then Print > Save as PDF).

| Report | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Executive Performance** | One-page summary for you or a meeting. Start here. |
| **Sales Transactions** | Every sale and its profit. Use it to check any number. |
| **Campaign Performance** | Spend, orders, revenue and ROAS per campaign. |
| **Product Profitability** | Which products and brands earn the most per item. |
| **Costs and Expenses** | Where the money went. |
| **Data Quality and Reconciliation** | Self-check. Every line should say "ok". If not, tell me the line. |

---

## 8. Monthly routine (30 minutes)

1. Enter the month's operating expenses (Finance, form 6).
2. Open Executive Performance for last month. Save the Excel or PDF in a folder named by month.
3. Read Product Profitability: source more of what earns the most per item, stop what earns little.
4. Read the Data Quality report. Everything must be "ok".

## 9. For a board or investor meeting

Bring the Executive Performance report plus these answers, each with its number:
- Revenue, profit after ads, margin, and the change from last month.
- Spend by channel and what each campaign returned (ROAS, cost per order).
- Best and worst products by profit.
- Refund rate and cash-on-delivery failures.
- Which costs are still not entered (be honest about what is estimated).
- The decisions you want approved (budget, stock to buy, campaigns to scale or stop).

## 10. When something looks wrong

| You see | Meaning | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping & Fulfillment = 0.00 | No order costs entered | Enter them in Finance, form 3 |
| Net Profit shows a dash | No operating expenses entered | Finance, form 6 |
| ROAS shows a dash | No spend in the period | Check the Ad Spend page |
| Many sales "unknown source" | Customers came through WhatsApp/Instagram chats | Normal. Ask buyers where they found you and record it (form 5) |
| "Meta Ads is not connected" | Credentials missing | Add `META_AD_ACCOUNT_ID` and `META_ACCESS_TOKEN` in Vercel |
| Spend lower than Ads Manager | Meta finalises numbers late | Press Sync now with 7 days |
| Revenue looks too low | Sales not marked completed or delivered | Check Finance, form 1 |

---

## 11. Glossary

- **Revenue**: money customers paid.
- **Gross profit**: revenue minus product cost.
- **Margin**: profit as a percentage of revenue.
- **Contribution**: profit after specific costs but before the fixed monthly ones.
- **Operating expenses**: monthly costs that do not belong to one order (rent, tools).
- **ROAS**: revenue divided by ad spend.
- **COD**: cash on delivery.
- **Attribution**: which ad or channel a sale came from.
